## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an ultra-minimalist, reductionist aesthetic tailored specifically for friction-free financial tracking. Designed for disciplined individuals who demand extreme clarity and rapid interaction, the interface removes all decorative ornament to spotlight numbers and input cadence. 

The aesthetic draws from Swiss Modernism and high-end instrument ergonomics: deep black canvases, surgical hairline borders, high-contrast typography, and subdued secondary zinc values. Every touchpoint feels immediate, tactile, and mathematically precise, transforming mundane expense logging into an effortless daily ritual.

## Colors

The palette is strictly monochromatic and rooted in a pure dark mode space.

- **Background Base**: `#000000` (deep absolute black canvas) and `#09090b` (foundation level).
- **Surface Elevation**: `#121214` (recessed inputs, underlying track plates) and `#18181b` (elevated cards, modal sheets).
- **Borders & Dividers**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` for razor-thin hairline structural division. Active states and focused strokes step up to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.24)`.
- **Content Hierarchy**:
  - **Primary**: `#ffffff` reserved exclusively for prominent financial figures, active input states, and primary action signifiers.
  - **Secondary**: `#a1a1aa` for standard descriptions, axis marks, and secondary actions.
  - **Tertiary / Muted**: `#71717a` for timestamps, metadata, inactive states, and category tags.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes extreme legibility, geometric discipline, and optical balance:

- **Geist** drives all narrative content, headers, and UI signifiers. It delivers clean apertures, subtle industrial character, and precise rendering on OLED displays. Tight negative letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) ensures a compact, editorial footprint.
- **JetBrains Mono** is deployed for numerical figures, transaction values, dates, and micro-labels. By enforcing fixed glyph widths and tabular figure presentation (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`), columns align seamlessly across rows, facilitating instant cognitive scanning of balances and sums.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a fluid single-column model on mobile devices to maximize vertical input speed, scaling into a structured 12-column fixed-max grid (max 720px for dedicated single-stream logging; max 1120px for expanded analytics screens) on desktop interfaces.

- **Mobile (<640px)**: 16px screen edge margins (`margin: 1rem`) ensure thumb reach across key action surfaces. Elements stack sequentially with `space-sm` (8px) or `space-md` (12px) separation.
- **Tablet / Desktop (≥640px)**: Margins expand progressively (`margin-tablet`, `margin-desktop`), centering the core entry apparatus.
- **Rhythm**: Component padding maintains strict 4px/8px alignment to sustain a compact, dense density profile without visual congestion.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed entirely through tonal stratification and hairline boundaries rather than dramatic drop shadows.

- **Layer 0 (Canvas)**: True `#000000` ground layer.
- **Layer 1 (Containers & List Cells)**: Flat fill `#09090b` or `#121214` bounded by a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 2 (Overlays & Quick-Log Drawers)**: Surface `#18181b` backed by subtle structural ambient shadow (`box-shadow: 0 16px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`), accompanied by a 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` top/outer edge highlight.
- **Focus Elements**: Interactive targets brighten their interior fill or step boundary opacity up to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.28)` without changing their dimensional footprint.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced curves to counter the stark contrast of the dark palette:

- Standard interactive controls, list containers, and entry rows employ `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px) or `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px).
- Modals, large summary cards, and primary container shields leverage `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for a soft framing appearance.
- Fast-action selectors, tags, and category chips utilize fully rounded contours (`rounded-full`) to offer clear tactile contrast against rectangular content blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#ffffff` background with `#000000` text, medium weight, `rounded-full` or `rounded-lg`. Zero shadow. Hover state scales down brightness to `#e4e4e7`.
- **Secondary**: Surface `#18181b` with `#ffffff` text, 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Active state shifts border to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.24)`.
- **Ghost / Utility**: Transparent background, `#a1a1aa` text. Transitions to `#ffffff` text and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` fill on press.

### Expense Input Fields (Quick-Log Pad)
- High-priority numeric entry field with transparent background, displaying currency prefix in muted `#71717a` and digits in high-contrast `#ffffff` using `JetBrains Mono` (`numeric-hero`).
- No underline or bottom border; the sheer scale of the typographic element commands the primary interaction focus.
- Metadata and category input inputs are enclosed within `#121214` containers featuring 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` borders and `rounded-lg` radius.

### Chips & Filters
- Compact pill-shaped elements (`rounded-full`) with padding of `space-xs` vertically and `space-md` horizontally.
- Unselected: Inactive fill `#09090b`, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#71717a`.
- Selected: Subtle white tint fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, crisp white border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.24)`, text `#ffffff`.

### Transaction Lists
- Border-separated or gap-spaced rows using `#121214` background cards.
- Left-aligned title (`Geist`, `#ffffff`, `body-md`) stacked over timestamp/category (`Geist`, `#71717a`, `body-sm`).
- Right-aligned amount rendered in `JetBrains Mono` (`numeric-table`), with `#ffffff` for standard outflows and subtle `#a1a1aa` for recurring entries.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles
- Custom minimal toggles embedded into hairline-framed enclosures. 
- Selection indicators employ stark `#ffffff` pips or background sliders moving smoothly across dark zinc wells.

### Quick-Log Floating Action Dock
- A pinned bottom navigation and capture dock floating over canvas with backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md`, background `rgba(9, 9, 11, 0.85)`), held together by a 1px boundary of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` and `rounded-full` contour.
